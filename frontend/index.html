How zero-knowledge verification protects your privacy

Private (never leaves your browser)

  • Your birth year
  • Your name and documents
  • The proof witness

Public (visible on chain)

  • Your wallet address
  • The proof and 3 public signals: result, current year, age limit
  • The time of verification

Limits of this prototype

  • A proof shows a statement is true for the input. It does not show the input is real.
  • A real system needs a trusted issuer to sign your birth year.
  • Your wallet address links all your records.
